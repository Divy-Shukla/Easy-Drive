Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to rent a car?

A valid driver's license, government-issued photo ID, and a payment method for security deposit.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

Yes, you can easily modify or cancel your booking through WhatsApp or by contacting our support team.

How does WhatsApp booking work?

Clicking any WhatsApp button pre-fills your request so our team or automated assistant can instantly check vehicle availability for your dates.
